Charging Problems We Fix

Most charging issues can be fixed quickly and affordably – no device replacement needed

Device Won't Charge

Plug in the cable and nothing happens? Could be a damaged port, broken pins, or corrosion. We diagnose and fix the exact issue.

Loose Cable Connection

Cable falls out or only charges at certain angles? The port pins are likely worn or bent. We replace the entire charging assembly.

Slow Charging

Device takes hours to charge? Could be a partially damaged port, dirty contacts, or a failing battery. Free diagnosis included.

Intermittent Charging

Charges sometimes but not always? Loose internal connections or corroded pins. We repair or replace the port for reliable charging.

Lint & Debris Buildup

Pocket lint and dust can block the connection. We professionally clean the port – often solving the issue without parts replacement.

Water Damage to Port

Liquid exposure can corrode charging pins. We clean corrosion and replace damaged components to restore charging.
