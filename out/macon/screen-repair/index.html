Types of Screen Damage We Repair

Cracked Glass

The most common damage from drops or impacts. We replace the entire glass assembly for a seamless finish.

Shattered Screen

Completely broken or spider-webbed screens. Even if pieces are missing, we can replace the full display.

LCD / OLED Damage

Black spots, lines, or discoloration on the display. We replace the full LCD or OLED panel for perfect visuals.

Touch Not Responding

Screen won't register touches or has ghost touches. A new digitizer restores full touch functionality.

Screen Lifting

Screen pulling away from the frame, often caused by a swollen battery. We fix both the screen and battery.

Water Damage Display

Water spots, flickering, or dim areas after liquid exposure. Screen replacement plus water damage treatment.
