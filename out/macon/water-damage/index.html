Dropped Your Device in Water? Do This NOW

Follow these emergency steps immediately – then bring it to Tech Medics

1

Remove It

Get it out of the water immediately. Every second counts.

2

Power OFF

Turn it off NOW. Do NOT try to charge it or use it.

3

Remove Case

Take off the case and remove the SIM card tray.

4

Shake Gently

Gently shake out excess water from ports and openings.

5

Skip the Rice

Rice is a MYTH. It doesn't help and can cause more damage.

6

Bring It In

Types of Liquid Damage We Repair

Pool & Ocean Water

Chlorine and salt water are especially corrosive. We clean all mineral deposits and corrosion from internal components.

Toilet & Sink Drops

The most common water damage scenario. Quick treatment usually results in full recovery.

Rain & Puddle Exposure

Even brief rain exposure can cause internal damage. Don't assume your device is fine – bring it in for inspection.

Coffee & Beverage Spills

Sugary drinks are especially damaging due to sticky residue. Professional cleaning removes all contaminants.

Washing Machine

Full submersion in soapy water. Challenging but we've saved many devices from the washing machine.

Condensation & Humidity

Moisture buildup from humid environments or temperature changes. Can cause slow corrosion over time.

Why Rice Doesn't Work

The “put it in rice” myth is one of the most damaging pieces of tech advice out there. Rice does NOT absorb moisture from inside your device. It simply wastes precious time while corrosion continues to eat away at your device's components. Worse, rice dust and starch can get into your ports and cause additional problems. Professional water damage treatment is the only reliable solution.
