Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a minimum or maximum repair amount for Afterpay?

Afterpay generally works for purchases between $1 and $2,000. Most of our repairs fall well within this range.

What happens if I miss a payment?

Afterpay may charge a late fee (capped at 25% of the order value). They'll send reminders before each payment is due so you never forget.

Do I need good credit to use Afterpay?

No. Afterpay does a soft check that doesn't affect your credit score. Most customers are approved instantly.

Can I use Afterpay for any repair?

Yes — iPhone screen repair, battery replacement, MacBook fixes, game console repairs, and accessories. Anything we sell or service at our shop.

How do I sign up?

Download the Afterpay app or tell our technician at checkout that you'd like to pay with Afterpay. Setup takes about 2 minutes.
