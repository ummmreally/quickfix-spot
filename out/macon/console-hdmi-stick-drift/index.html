Console HDMI Port Repair & Controller Stick Drift Fix in Macon, Georgia

If your PS5 or Xbox has no video output, a broken HDMI port is almost certainly the cause. And if your controller character moves on its own, analog stick drift needs a hardware fix – not a software reset. Tech Medics in Macon, GA specializes in both of these extremely common gaming repairs.

PS5 HDMI Port Repair in Macon, GA

The PlayStation 5 HDMI port is a surface-mounted connector soldered directly to the motherboard. When bent pins, a pulled cable, or accidental damage breaks the port, the console powers on but sends no signal to the TV. Replacing a PS5 HDMI port requires micro-soldering – carefully desoldering the damaged connector, inspecting the solder pads for lifted traces, and installing a brand-new HDMI port. At Tech Medics, our technicians have the precision equipment and experience to complete this repair reliably. We test at 1080p and 4K before returning your console.

Xbox Series X & Series S HDMI Repair in Macon

Xbox Series X HDMI port damage presents the same symptoms – power light comes on, but the TV shows no signal. The HDMI connector on Xbox consoles is equally delicate and requires the same micro-soldering process. We repair Xbox Series X, Xbox Series S, Xbox One, and Xbox One X HDMI ports at our Macon shop. This is one of our most requested repairs, and we carry replacement HDMI connectors in stock for fast turnaround.

Joy-Con Drift Repair – Nintendo Switch

Joy-Con drift is arguably the most widespread controller defect in gaming history. The Nintendo Switch analog stick uses a potentiometer-based sensor that wears down over time, causing phantom inputs. Your character walks without you touching the stick, menus scroll on their own, and aiming becomes impossible. We replace the entire Joy-Con analog stick module – not just clean it – for a lasting fix. This repair applies to Switch, Switch OLED, and Switch Lite models.

DualSense & Xbox Controller Stick Drift Repair

The PS5 DualSense controller and Xbox Series controller both use Alps-style analog stick potentiometers that can develop drift within months of use. Symptoms include aim wobble in FPS games, characters pulling to one side, and inconsistent camera movement. We desolder the faulty potentiometer and replace it with a fresh module, restoring precise analog input. This same process applies to the Xbox Elite Series 2 controller, which is especially frustrating given its premium price point.

Steam Deck Stick Drift Repair

The Steam Deck by Valve uses hall-effect capable thumbstick modules, but drift can still occur. Whether your Steam Deck stick is drifting, has dead zones, or the click function has stopped working, we carry replacement modules and can repair it at our Macon shop.
