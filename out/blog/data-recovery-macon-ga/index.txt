1c:T1d9a,<h2>Don't Lose Your Irreplaceable Data</h2>
<p>Your device holds years of photos, important contacts, work documents, and memories that can't be replaced. When your iPhone screen shatters, your MacBook won't boot, or your device takes a swim – your data doesn't have to be lost forever. At Tech Medics Macon, we specialize in recovering data from damaged, broken, and non-functional devices using professional tools and techniques that go far beyond what you can do at home.</p>
<h3>When Do You Need Data Recovery?</h3>
<p>Data recovery is needed whenever you can't access the files on your device through normal means. Here are the most common scenarios we handle:</p>
<ul><li><strong>Broken or shattered screen:</strong> Your iPhone screen is completely destroyed – you can't see anything, can't enter your passcode, and can't back up to iCloud. The data is still there on the storage chip; you just can't access it through the broken display.</li><li><strong>Water damage:</strong> Your device won't turn on after being submerged or splashed. <a href="/blog/water-damaged-iphone-repair">Water damage</a> can corrode connections to the storage, but the storage chip itself is often intact.</li><li><strong>Dead device:</strong> Phone or laptop is completely unresponsive – no screen, no vibration, no charging indicator. Could be a failed battery, logic board, or other component, but your data may still be recoverable.</li><li><strong>Failed iOS or macOS update:</strong> An update got stuck, corrupted the operating system, or the device is stuck on the Apple logo or recovery mode screen.</li><li><strong>Hard drive failure:</strong> MacBook making clicking sounds, showing a flashing folder with a question mark, or extremely slow to respond. The drive may be failing but data can often be extracted before total failure.</li><li><strong>Accidental deletion:</strong> Deleted important photos, messages, or files. Depending on the device and how quickly you act, recovery may be possible.</li><li><strong>Forgotten passcode:</strong> Locked out of your device with no backup. In some cases, we can help recover data depending on the device and iOS version.</li></ul>
<h3>Our Data Recovery Process</h3>
<p>At Tech Medics Macon, we follow a systematic approach to maximize your chances of successful recovery:</p>
<ol><li><strong>Free assessment (same day):</strong> We examine your device, determine the type and extent of damage, and give you an honest assessment of recovery chances and cost. No obligation, no pressure.</li><li><strong>Device stabilization:</strong> If the device has water damage, we clean and stabilize it first to prevent further corrosion. If the screen is broken, we may connect a test display to access the device.</li><li><strong>Storage access:</strong> Depending on the situation, we may repair the device enough to access the storage, connect directly to the storage chip using specialized tools, or boot the device in diagnostic modes that allow data extraction.</li><li><strong>Data extraction:</strong> We extract your files to a secure external drive. We can selectively recover specific types of data (just photos, for example) or do a complete extraction of everything.</li><li><strong>Verification:</strong> We verify the recovered data with you to make sure everything you need has been successfully extracted.</li><li><strong>Secure handoff:</strong> Your recovered data is transferred to a USB drive, external hard drive, or cloud service of your choice.</li></ol>
<h3>What Can We Recover?</h3>
<p>Nearly everything that was stored on your device:</p>
<ul><li><strong>Photos and videos:</strong> Camera roll, screenshots, downloaded images, and videos – including those not backed up to iCloud</li><li><strong>Contacts:</strong> Your entire contact list with phone numbers, emails, and addresses</li><li><strong>Text messages and iMessages:</strong> Complete message history including attachments</li><li><strong>Notes:</strong> All your Apple Notes content</li><li><strong>Documents:</strong> PDFs, Word docs, spreadsheets, and other files stored on the device</li><li><strong>App data:</strong> Data from specific apps including WhatsApp messages, health data, and more</li><li><strong>Voice memos:</strong> Audio recordings stored on the device</li><li><strong>Calendar and reminders:</strong> All scheduled events and to-do items</li></ul>
<h3>iPhone Data Recovery</h3>
<p>iPhone data recovery is our most requested service. iPhones store data on encrypted NAND flash storage, which makes recovery more complex than traditional hard drives. However, our specialized tools can access this storage in many scenarios:</p>
<ul><li><strong>Broken screen + known passcode:</strong> High success rate. We can often connect a test display or use accessibility features to unlock and back up the device.</li><li><strong>Water damage:</strong> Success depends on the extent of corrosion. <a href="/macon/water-damage">Quick professional treatment</a> significantly improves recovery chances.</li><li><strong>Won't turn on:</strong> If the storage chip is intact, we can often <a href="/blog/iphone-not-turning-on-fix">repair enough components</a> to access the data even if the phone can't be fully restored.</li></ul>
<h3>MacBook Data Recovery</h3>
<p>MacBook data recovery varies by model and issue:</p>
<ul><li><strong>Older MacBooks with removable drives:</strong> We can remove the SSD or hard drive and read it directly – high success rate.</li><li><strong>Newer MacBooks with soldered storage:</strong> More complex, but we can often boot from external media to access the internal storage.</li><li><strong>Liquid damage:</strong> Logic board repair may be needed to access the storage. <a href="/blog/macbook-battery-issues-swelling-macon">Learn about MacBook damage repair</a>.</li><li><strong>Clicking hard drives:</strong> Mechanical failure in older MacBooks. Recovery is possible but may require specialized equipment.</li></ul>
